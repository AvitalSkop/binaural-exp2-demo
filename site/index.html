Binaural HRTF-Conditioned Target Speaker Extraction

A diffusion model is given a binaural two-speaker mixture and the HRTF of a requested direction, and aims to extract that speaker while reducing reverberation. Five variants differ only in how the HRTF conditions the network.

Binaural spatial perception should be evaluated using headphones.
